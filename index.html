How to Read This Clock

The famous Doomsday Clock measures how close the world is to midnight. This one borrows the idea for a happier ending: midnight is the moment the baby arrives, and every setting between here and there is a stage on the way.

A calendar cannot set these hands — the Board moves them when the phase changes, and until a new phase arrives, the clock stays exactly where it is: 11:42 PM, eighteen minutes to midnight.

This will update accordingly whenever the hands move — the next section explains what each new setting means.
